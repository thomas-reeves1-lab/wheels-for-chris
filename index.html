This is not the start of the fundraiser. It is the last bit of it.

Chris lives in Ulverstone and has cerebral palsy. A while back this town got behind a GoFundMe and got him a van, and it changed his week. Appointments, the shops, the footy, a mate's place. Getting out the front door stopped being somebody else's schedule.

The catch was the budget. It stretched to a cheap van, and a cheap van has been exactly that. It keeps breaking down. Every breakdown is another week of Chris stuck waiting on a lift.

The NDIS does not fund vehicles, and he has no equipment funding to put toward one. So this raffle is the last stretch: enough to get him into something reliable and keep it on the road. Chris is running it himself, with his support workers and a few other mates chipping in.

The straight version

Things we will tell you whether you ask or not

  • Your odds. There is no fixed number of tickets. The total sold, and what that makes your odds, gets posted on the morning of the draw before anything is pulled.
  • Where the money goes. Every prize is donated, so prizes cost the raffle nothing. Every dollar after fees goes to Chris for the van.
  • Who holds it. The money goes to Chris. This is his fundraiser, run by him with people who know him.
  • If we fall short. Whatever is raised still goes to Chris toward the vehicle. It does not get refunded and it does not get spent on anything else.

Questions

The usual ones

How many tickets are there?

No cap. Tickets sell right up to the draw, so the more the town buys, the more goes to the van. The trade off is honest: more tickets sold means longer odds on each one, which is why the total gets posted before the draw.

How do I get my ticket numbers?

They come back to you as soon as you pay, to the email or phone you used. If nothing turns up within a day, email Chris and he will chase it.

Do I have to be there for the draw?

No. Winners get rung, and the results go up publicly the same day.

Didn't Chris already get a van?

Yes, and this town paid for it. The budget only stretched to a cheap one and it has been unreliable since. This raffle is the last stretch to get him into something that starts every time.

Can I donate without buying a ticket?

Yes. Tick the $10 box in the buy section, or email Chris and he will point you at the straight donation option.

I want to donate a prize instead.

Even better. Email Chris and your gift card goes on the prize board with your business named. The pool grows, more tickets sell, and everyone wins except the cheap van.

Is this an official raffle?

Yes. It runs under Tasmania's minor gaming rules, with the permit or exemption details recorded by the organisers and available on request.
