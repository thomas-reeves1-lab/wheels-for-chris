Wheels for Chris · Design brief

Bridie's Design Kit

Five assets, one consistent look, reused everywhere. Get the ticket right first — it's the whole campaign's visual identity in miniature, and everything else follows from it.

01

Palette

Think old raffle-ticket-book colours: cream paper, teal ink, a gold number, a red stamp for the date. Warm and local, like a shop counter — not bright party-balloon colours.

Ink
#1B2A2F
Paper
#F5F1E6
Teal
#2F6F6A
Gold
#B5811F
Stamp red
#A5432A

Stamp red is for urgency only — draw date, countdown, "TODAY". Don't spread it everywhere.

02

Type

Headlines
Fraunces — names, prize amounts
Numbers / stubs / prices
IBM Plex Mono — $10, No. 001
Body copy
Work Sans — easy to read on a phone

All three are free on Google Fonts — search each by name in Canva's font picker.

03

Asset 1 — The ticket

Ticket
Roughly business-card / A6, two-up or four-up on A4 and cut.

Must include: ticket number, "Wheels for Chris", price, draw date, one line on the prize, and a note that odds will be posted on draw day.

WHEELS FOR CHRIS Ticket No. ___ $10 single · 3 for $25 · 6 for $45 Drawn [date] at [event] 4 prize packs · ~$1,000 in local gift cards Odds posted morning of the draw
04

Asset 2 — Hero launch post

Hero post
1080×1080 feed, 1080×1920 Story crop.

Must include: one strong photo of Chris (with his OK), his story in under 40 words, the ask, ticket price, where to buy.

Chris has cerebral palsy. NDIS doesn't fund vehicles, and he has no AT funding for one either. A van means getting around Ulverstone on his own terms. $10 a ticket. 4 local prize packs worth ~$1,000. Drawn [date]. Grab a ticket at [businesses] or link in bio.
05

Asset 3 — Prize board

Prize board
1080×1350, tall feed post.

All four packs laid out like a scoreboard, with real photos of the actual gift cards once assembled (see the organiser's runbook, Step 3).

1ST — THE BIG ONE — ~$400 2ND — ULVERSTONE LOCAL PACK — ~$300 3RD — NIGHT OUT PACK — ~$200 EARLY BIRD — ~$100 (drawn [date], before the main draw)
06

Asset 4 — Countdown template

Countdown
1080×1920 Story.

Build it once with one swappable number field — "12 DAYS TO THE DRAW", "5 DAYS", "TODAY" — so reposting is a five-minute edit, not a new design each time. Use the stamp red for the number.

07

Asset 5 — Winner card

Winner card
1080×1080, one template, four uses.

The early bird winner, then each of the three main draw winners. Same frame every time so the campaign reads as one thread from open to close.

CONGRATULATIONS [NAME]! Winner of [pack name] Thanks to everyone who bought a ticket — $[total] raised for Chris's van.
Companion document: Run It & Call — the organiser's runbook, including the call to make before any tickets go on sale.
